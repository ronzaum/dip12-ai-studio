# Unit consultants

Voices the unit suggests, shared by everyone in Diploma 12. Your own cards sit one level up, in `expertise/`, and are yours alone. This folder is the unit's.

**One card per name** on the unit's list: the reading list plus wildcards. Each card quotes the person's own words with page or timestamp; the raw material sits in `expertise/sources/<slug>/`. The full list and each card's status is in `expertise/INDEX.md` under "Unit consultants".

**Status.** Every name starts as `suggested by unit`. `verified` means every quote has been checked against its saved source. `unverified` means something on it still needs checking.

**How `/consult` uses it.** It reads the list in `INDEX.md`, opens only the cards that fit your question, and labels each voice `(unit)` or `(yours)`.

**Make one yours.** `/gather promote <name>` copies the card into `expertise/`, sets its status, adds it to your shortlist and logs the decision. The unit card stays here for everyone else.

Don't edit the unit cards themselves. Promote, then edit your copy.
