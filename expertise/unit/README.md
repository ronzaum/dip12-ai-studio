# Unit consultants

Voices the unit suggests, shared by everyone in Diploma 12. Your own 3/3/3, design and practice cards sit in the other folders and are yours alone. This folder is the unit's.

- **`_brief.md`**: the Book of Rituals brief as a voice: what the unit asks of your project, quoted from `project/brief-dip12.md`.
- **One card per name** on the unit's list: the unit's reading list plus wildcards. Each card quotes the person's own words with page or timestamp, and its raw material sits in `expertise/sources/<slug>/`. The full list and each card's status is in `expertise/INDEX.md` under "Unit consultants".

**Status.** Every name starts as `suggested by unit`. A card marked `verified` has had every quote checked against its saved source. `unverified` means something on it still needs checking.

**How `/consult` uses it.** Every run reads all the unit cards and all of yours, then picks the voices that fit your question. Each voice is labelled `(unit)` or `(yours)` so you can see where it came from.

**Make one yours.** If a unit consultant becomes central to your project, promote it: `/gather promote <name>`. Claude copies the card into your own area (`texts/`, `experts/`, `practices/` …), sets its status, adds it to your shortlist in `INDEX.md`, and logs the decision. The unit card stays here for everyone else.

Don't edit the unit cards themselves. Promote, then edit your copy.
