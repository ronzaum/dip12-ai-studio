## Positions
- "As I start a project, I always need to create a world. Then I want to enter this world and my walk through this world is the work." (Art21, "Romance", 2007, 00:00:10) — in `2007-art21-romance-segment.txt`
- "What is important for me is to create holes in the map. Put white on the map. By doing so you bring back some myth, create zone of non-knowledge. Blur certitude." (Art21, "Romance", 2007, 00:05:18) — in `2007-art21-romance-segment.txt`
- "Then people play this mythology in the form of a party which we call celebration, one time a year, we have a score, and then you have an interpretation." (Art21, "Romance", 2007, 00:13:32) — in `2007-art21-romance-segment.txt`

## How they work
No studio: "I never had a place where things are made"; he starts with notes, "a table, a piece of paper, a pen" (Art Section). Writes a "conditional set up", a program, instructions, then steps back and lets the thing form (Art Section). Four people in his own studio, about twenty outside specialists; collaborators are "a tool to deregulate fixity of the self" (Elephant). Untilled took the compost's method: things are thrown on the pile, not placed (Louisiana, 00:05:22–00:05:57). Works are revised; "the exhibition is not the end of a process, but is the starting point to go somewhere else" (Art21, 00:14:18).

## Hooks to the brief
- **Sacred / profane:** Untilled sits in the compost, "the back of the park", where a park throws things out; he throws "element of History" in: a Beuys oak, a statue whose head is a beehive (Louisiana, 00:05:22–00:06:57).
- **Ritual:** Streamside Day was a celebration he organised, parade to mayor's speech, then wrote as "a score" so the town can "come back and come back" each 11 October (Art21, 00:12:28–00:13:09).
- **Time:** He wants "to produce a time, the duration that will just come back in time" (Art21, 00:13:06); not a loop but "a cycle that includes change" (Art Section).
- **Non-human:** In After ALife Ahead the augmented reality "is no more important than the ants that no one sees" (Elephant); at Untilled the bees and the dog ignore the public.
- **Place:** "It is a place where an uncertain ecology might occur, [but] having a dramatic place wasn’t a goal. It is not about landscape." (Elephant). He picks an enclosed but porous site, then lets it leak.
- **Power:** "I always thought humor was a way to broke the rule of power." (Art21, 00:09:41). His politics is "an organization, as a distribution of goods or ideas among a spectrum of people", never propaganda (Art Section).
