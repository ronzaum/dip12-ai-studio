## How they work
Writes from reading and from small observed events, not fieldwork: a man on a Beijing park bench, strangers on a Greyhound bus (letter, 26 March 2010). Sits in the State Street Starbucks as "my listening post" to hear students (UW–Madison, 2013). Wrote a letter to colleagues most weeks for decades; over 700 are archived (about page, 2008). Takes a question and turns it four ways: a genre of story and a unit of land; fear; the moral content of folklore; whether the bond with place is an illusion (AFS talk, 00:09:11 to 00:10:17). Draws on diaries, novels and Chinese and European painting as evidence. Describes his own method as finding the "objective correlative" of a feeling, borrowed from T. S. Eliot: for him, the desert (Olwig conversation, 00:17:45 to 00:18:10). Books over twenty, written alone; the archive of letters built with Melanie McCalmont and the UW–Madison Geography Library.

## Hooks to the brief
- **Sacred / profane:** Place is known through "the proximate senses of touch smell and taste" while landscape "is primarily a construction of the dominant sense of sight" seen "from a point above ground level" (AFS talk, 00:12:01 to 00:13:11). Decide whether your ritual is lived in place or viewed as landscape, and draw it that way.
- **Ritual:** Stories "even as they reduced fear also revealed its extent" (AFS talk, 00:21:59). Record what the ritual at your site keeps at bay.
- **Time:** Fear in place "has diminished in our time" (AFS talk, 00:09:37); the timeline can show what a place stopped being afraid of, and when.
- **Non-human:** The world's "fundamental indifference" to us (AFS talk, 00:47:06); a desert as the "objective correlative of who i am" (Olwig, 00:18:07). A non-human stakeholder need not love you back.
- **Place:** "folklore is anchored in place or locality" (AFS talk, 00:16:46). Collect the stories told about your exact location.
- **Power:** The Greek private person was an idiot; the citizen stood "in the city's forum" (letter, 6 July 2010). Map who in your conflict is allowed to be public.
