## How they work
Starts from stories that already exist in the Ends and treats the work as archiving: "documents the lives and times of people who live in my imagination, both in reality and in fantasy". Looks for new forms when the old ones cannot hold the material: the way people talk in Peckham "can't necessarily be contained in iambic pentameter". Shoots his own black-and-white photographs and sets them against the poems to counter the news images. *The Wickedest* was built as one night with the pacing of a party; he wrote it in different cities while working on a TV set, and mixed in photos, Notes-app messages and timestamps because "a good party is so multifaceted". The idea grew out of lockdown conversations with Virgil Abloh about the lineage of parties (David Mancuso). Poetry, photography and film are one practice to him: "they all try to capture a perspective".

## Hooks to the brief
- **Sacred / profane:** The party as a space to protect from outsiders: the interviewer sums up the book's ending as sending the reader out with "just don't tell anyone about this" (2025 interview, interviewer speaking, auto-captions, 00:15:21–00:15:29); the party is guarded, not advertised.
- **Ritual:** A shoobs as a repeating rite: "the longest running house party in the South London shoob scene, always held at an undisclosed inner-city spot" (US publisher description, saved); the function of the party is where a community "exchange human information" (2025 interview, auto-captions, 00:02:59).
- **Time:** One night at one party, curated hour by hour: "there's a science behind a successful night" (2025 interview, auto-captions, 00:08:51); a direct model for a time-based drawing of a ritual.
- **Place:** "the North Peckham Estate: 65 stories, 1,444 homes", linked by corridors so "you don't necessarily need to leave the estate" (2020 Complex interview); the roads as the place where you gather when the flat has seven people in one bedroom (2017 talk, 00:06:46).
- **Power:** Gentrification as "the loss of landmarks"; gated communities now stand where the estate was (2020 Complex interview).
