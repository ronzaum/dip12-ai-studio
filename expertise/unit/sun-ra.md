## How they work
Words first. The Berkeley lecture opens with etymology: he reads the words of the Bible, hymns and want ads back to their roots and treats the result as "equations" (pt 1, 00:00:16 on; 1979 talk, 00:00:02). He marked up scripture with a red pencil "just like I would any other book" (pt 1, 00:12:56). The band is the method: a group living and rehearsing together under discipline, in a New York apartment in 1966 (Sinclair interview, intro). Names change by dimension, not by decision: Myth-Science, Solar, Astro-Infinity Arkestra (Sinclair). Music is defined wide: a painting with symmetry is music, "that's what music is all about feeling" (pt 1, 00:10:09). The planet is an instrument out of tune; the tuner is a master musician (pt 2, 00:13:22). Time: a university course, a 73-minute talk, an hour's interview; the same equations repeated for decades.

## Hooks to the brief
- **Sacred / profane:** He takes the pledge card and the collection plate as the real contract of a church: "sign your name put your money in there", and the vow is binding only while the money is (pt 1, 00:19:11–00:20:16). A ritual's technology can be paper and coins.
- **Ritual:** Hymn lines ("where shall I be when the first trumpet sound") are read as instructions about sound and waking the dead (pt 2, 00:03:28–00:04:27). A ritual is a set formula of words, like the Lord's Prayer (pt 1, 00:03:51–00:04:05).
- **Time:** "one age moves over into another one" and the law of the planet changes with it (Sinclair). The project is the change of age, not the fix inside it.
- **Non-human:** The music of the spheres taken literally: every planet is a sphere with its own note, and this one is out of tune (pt 2, 00:13:22–00:14:02). The stakeholder can be the planet.
- **Place:** Oakland in *Space Is the Place*; the Arkestra's shared house as the site of discipline (Sinclair intro).
- **Power:** "the rulers on this planet are in trouble" and he sympathises with them (Sinclair). Freedom is refused as a goal; discipline and schools of discipline are proposed instead (1979, 00:15:43).
