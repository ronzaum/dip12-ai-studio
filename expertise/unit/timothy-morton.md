## Positions
- "tectonic plates global warming nuclear radiation evolution these are hyperobjects entities that are massively distributed in time and space at least relative to human scales" ("Dawn of the Hyperobjects", 2011, 00:00:23–00:00:35) — in `2011-dawn-of-the-hyperobjects-talk.txt`
- "there is no exit from this situation thus the time of hyperobject is a time of sincerity that is a time in which it's impossible to achieve a final distance towards the world" ("Dawn of the Hyperobjects", 2011, 00:12:36–00:12:48) — in `2011-dawn-of-the-hyperobjects-talk.txt`
- "global warming spells the end of the world not in an apocalypse but in an abject living on in the absence of a meaningful life world bounded by a distant Horizon" ("Dawn of the Hyperobjects", 2011, 00:37:34–00:37:46) — in `2011-dawn-of-the-hyperobjects-talk.txt`

## How they work
Quantity first, then detail. "If it's a book, 2000 words per day (or more if the spirit moves me), then stop and do anything else." Then "print, scribble, redo (repeat until you can't do it any more)." Writes in bed in the dark, with music on, with people around (New APPS, 2011). Off social media since 2016: "I’m not on social media." Thinks by talking: the hyperobject idea was built across a run of keynotes (2011). Collaborators are artists and musicians: Björk, Jennifer Walshe (libretto *Time, Time, Time*, 2019), Justin Guariglia, Andrew Melchior, Olafur Eliasson (about page; Guardian).

## Hooks to the brief
- **Sacred / profane:** *Hell* and the "Go To Hell" post read the European dream of paradise on earth as the machine that produced hell: "a concentration camp, plantation world." A ritual that promises purity is his target.
- **Time:** a hyperobject is "massively distributed in time", so the time-based drawing has to hold scales a film cannot: the plutonium half-life next to the car ignition.
- **Non-human:** the stakeholder diagram should hold "massive non-human non-sentient entities" (00:00:45) and the "beings, human or otherwise" he wants affiliations with.
- **Place:** eco means house: "the Earth is a house for us all" (00:32:01). The site is inside the object, not in front of it: "we discover ourselves on the inside of some big objects bigger than us."
