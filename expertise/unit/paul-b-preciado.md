## How they work
Writes short pieces first: 1,500-word newspaper columns (Libération, 2013–18) later collected as a book; magazine essays; a lecture to 3,500 psychoanalysts that became a book. Works from Foucault's method, then takes one built case and reads it as a laboratory of power: the Playboy Mansion and Hugh Hefner's rotating bed in *Pornotopia*, the family home and Virginia Woolf's room in the Gagosian conversation. Reads bodies the way others read buildings: skin, facade, temple, museum. Writes in French and Spanish; translators are named on every English edition (Molly Stevens, Charlotte Mandell, Frank Wynne, Bruce Benderson). Lectures and curates in parallel with writing (Reina Sofía's Somateca programme, Documenta 14).

## Hooks to the brief
- **Sacred / profane:** "In Christianity, the body is thought of as the temple (in a strict architectural sense) of the soul." (Gagosian Quarterly, 2020) The body is the first sacred building; the clinic, the anatomy theatre and the home profane or re-consecrate it.
- **Ritual:** "Torture and social blood rituals are forms of inscription of the law"; Woolf's room extracts her body "from the disciplinary rituals of reproduction and heterosexual domesticity" (both Gagosian Quarterly, 2020). A ritual, for him, is how law gets written on a body.
- **Time:** the Artforum essay (2020) is a timeline of epidemics (syphilis 1494, AIDS 1981, Covid 2020), each one changing how a community draws its border. Use it as a model for the When section.
- **Non-human:** he asks for "a parliament of (vulnerable) bodies living on planet Earth" and health as "a new understanding of community with all living creatures" (Artforum, 2020).
- **Place:** the home is the new cell: "The private residence has now become the center of the economy of tele-consumption and tele-production, but also the surveillance pod." (Artforum, 2020)
- **Power:** "The new frontier is your epidermis. The new Lampedusa is your skin." (Artforum, 2020) Border, mask and home are one apparatus.
