## How they work
Began in theatre, dance and BBC radio drama in 1950s London, then the novel, then essays in the Jamaica Journal after independence (KCL profile; IOJ response). One hypothesis, reworked over decades: in 2012 she is still writing "my still ongoing introduction to a collection of my essays" (00:00:55). Argues by long chains of citation across disciplines: Goveia, Geertz, Latour, Rorty, Césaire, Pagels, palaeontology. Works alone, by letter and long essay. Her papers are at Duke's Rubenstein Library.

## Hooks to the brief
- **Sacred / profane:** every culture runs on a "code of symbolic life and death" (00:10:58): ask which code the site's sacred space enforces, and who it marks as symbolically dead.
- **Ritual:** her generation turned "towards the very local cultural afro-west indian afro-jamaican attributes which had been despised and devalued" (00:02:35): the ritual you film may be one of the despised forms, not the official one.
- **Non-human:** "No Humans Involved" is about people filed outside the human; the brief's "non-human entity" can be read the other way round: who at the site is treated as not human.
- **Power:** the letter's "New Poor" has "no function" once consumption replaces production: a way to name the power behind the conflict in economic terms.
