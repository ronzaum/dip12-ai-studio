# Ritual

Evidence for the Ritual section of the Book of Rituals (`project/brief-dip12.md`): the one ritual that makes your conflict visible.

What goes here:
- **Film stills** from your ritual film and its research footage
- **Transcripts** of interviews, recordings and first-hand accounts
- **Time-based drawings** (required by the brief): how the ritual unfolds over time
- Diagrams, photos and documents on who enacts it, with what, where and why

Audio and video go in the book as stills and transcripts, so save those here, not only the clips. Export drawings as PDF or PNG so Claude can read them.
