# Book

Each week, photograph your physical research book laid out on the table. Save as `YYYY-MM-DD-01.jpg`. The book stays physical; this folder is its record.

Every photo gets a markdown next to it (`YYYY-MM-DD-01.md`), written by Claude when the photo lands: what is on the spread, what changed since the last one, and one open question to bring to your tutorial. Over the year those markdowns are the digital copy of the book: searchable, and something `/consult` and `/artifact` can read.
