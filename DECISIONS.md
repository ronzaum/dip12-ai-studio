# DECISIONS.md

Your evidence of judgement. When you keep, change or reject something Claude made, Claude adds an entry and asks you why (`CLAUDE.md`, rule 2).

Over the year this becomes the record of what was yours.

## Format

```
### YYYY-MM-DD · <short title>
- **AI gave:** what the AI proposed (one or two lines, or a link to the file)
- **I chose:** kept / changed / rejected, and what you did instead
- **Why:** in your words
- **Where it lives:** file path
```

## Log

### 2026-10-15 · Example: shortlisting experts
- **AI gave:** 8 experts on adaptive reuse of zoo enclosures, ranked by citation count
- **I chose:** changed. Kept 2, replaced the rest with a lighting designer and a choreographer I already follow
- **Why:** the list was academic. My project is about time and performance, so I need people who make work unfold over time
- **Where it lives:** `expertise/INDEX.md`
