You are running Superpower. The student is in the middle of making something and wants it more valuable, more clever, more worth looking at. Not bigger. Not more complex. Better.

## Steps
1. Look at what the student is working on: the file or page they name, or the most recently changed files in the repo. Read `project/brief.md` so the suggestions fit their project.
2. Suggest **3 options** to elevate it. Each should be:
   - something that makes a tutor think "this student gets it"
   - a quality, insight or value increase, not a scope increase
   - specific to this piece of work, not generic advice
3. Format each option as:

**Option N: short name**
- **What:** what you'd do, specific to the current work
- **Benefit:** what it improves for the viewer or the project
- **Value:** why it matters in the bigger picture
- **Why it's clever:** what makes this non-obvious

## Rules
- Exactly 3 options. Enough to choose from, not enough to overwhelm.
- Bias toward things that are visible. If a tutor is looking, they need to see it.
- Never suggest something that risks breaking what already works.
- Readable in 30 seconds. The student picks one and carries on.
- Do not suggest: more sources for their own sake, restructuring, or polish that hides the thinking.
- The student decides which, if any, to take. Log it per CLAUDE.md rule 2.
